❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from our customers

1. How can I place an order?

Call us on 0777 123 456 or send a message through our contact page. Tell us what product you want and quantity.

2. Do you deliver to Kampala?

Yes we deliver to Kampala and surrounding areas. Delivery fee depends on location.

3. Can I pick up products from Mukono?

Yes you can pick up from our office in Kyankima village, Mukono District. Call first to confirm availability.

4. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and cash on delivery.

5. Are your products authentic handmade?

Yes all crafts are handmade by our 47 members. Farm produce is grown by our members.

6. How do I become a member?

Contact us and we will guide you. New members are welcome especially women in Mukono.

7. Do you sell in bulk?

Yes bulk orders are available for vegetables, eggs, and crafts. Contact us for bulk prices.

8. Where is Kyankima located?

Kyankima is a village in Mukono District, about 30km from Kampala city.
