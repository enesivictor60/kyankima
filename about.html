📜 Our History

Kyankima Women's Cooperative was founded in 2019 by 47 women in Kyankima village, Mukono District. The women came together to support each other in small-scale agriculture and handcraft production. What started as a small savings group grew into a registered community-based organization.

In 2020, we received support from the local government to expand our farming activities. In 2022, we started the health awareness program with 3 volunteer health workers. Now in 2026, we are finally going online with our first website.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower women through sustainable farming, quality handcraft production, and community health awareness, while improving household incomes.

👁️ Our Vision

To become the leading women's cooperative in Mukono District known for quality products and community health impact.

🤝 Our Partners

  • 3 Community Health Workers - Provide monthly health tips
  • Mukono Local Government - Provided startup support
  • Local Markets in Kampala - Sell our products
  • HostAfrica - Domain registration support
