💚 Monthly Health Tips

From our partner health workers

👩‍⚕️ Tip from Nurse Sarah - March 2026

Topic: Hand washing prevents disease

Wash your hands with soap and running water before eating and after using the toilet. This simple habit prevents diarrhea and other infections. Teach your children to do the same.

👨‍⚕️ Tip from Dr. James - February 2026

Topic: Eating local vegetables

Local vegetables like nakati, dodo, and buga are rich in vitamins. They boost immunity and are cheaper than imported foods. Try to eat vegetables with every meal.

👩‍⚕️ Tip from Midwife Grace - January 2026

Topic: Prenatal care for mothers

Pregnant mothers should attend all antenatal visits. This helps detect problems early and ensures a safe delivery. Immunization of children is also very important.

📋 General Health Tips

  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily
  • Sleep 7-8 hours each night
  • Exercise for 30 minutes daily
  • Visit a health worker when you feel unwell
  • Keep your home environment clean
